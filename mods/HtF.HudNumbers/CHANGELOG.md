# Changelog

All notable changes to this package. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).


## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- Health, fullness, poison and fire as actual numbers.
- Money on hand.
- Held item: name, value, weight, cookedness, score multiplier.
- Crosshair target: health and stats of the creature or item you are looking at, out to a configurable range.
- Configurable corner, margins, scale and system font.
- Toggle key (F6 by default) and a hide-while-paused option.
- Bilingual UI, following the language setting of `HtF_ConfigMenu` or the game's own language.
